# Instructor guide

Notes for instructors teaching Train the Trainer. These files are not
part of the training website: participants don't see them there.

- [Modules](modules.md): notes for each module.
- The Community section has no teaching session: it points
  participants to the community of practice after the course.

## About the course

The course is based on the materials of the
[ELIXIR-GOBLET Train-the-Trainer community](https://elixir-europe.org/platforms/training/train-the-trainer).
It is highly interactive: participants are expected to take an active
part in all sessions. A maximum of 25 participants can attend.
